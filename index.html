AlarmChain privacy policy

App: AlarmChain (com.rasikhqadeer.alarmchain) · Effective 16 October 2026

Summary

AlarmChain collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

Your routines (names, steps and minutes), the run in progress, your settings and a short record of recent alarms are stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

What the app sends

Nothing. AlarmChain does not use the internet and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.

Permissions

  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): to ring each step at its exact time.
  • Notifications and full-screen alarm: to show the ringing screen over the lock screen, the countdown and the reminders.
  • Run at startup: to set your alarms again after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration, wake lock and foreground service: to vibrate and keep the alarm sound playing until you answer it.

None of these permissions is used to collect or share data.

Children

AlarmChain is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
